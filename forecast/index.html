    How it has done so far

    Since the service started issuing forecasts it has made 57 of them, 46 of which have an observation to be checked against. On those, the mean absolute error is 16.0 µg/m³ and the AQI band was right 22 times out of 46.

    That is the live record, not the backtest. It is short, and it only covers a quiet time of year. The full evaluation is the number to argue with.
