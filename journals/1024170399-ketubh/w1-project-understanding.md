# Week 1 : Understanding the Project Problem and Objective

Week one was mainly focused on understanding the problem statement, motivation, and overall objective of our semester project, Pawan.

The project aims to provide a locally calibrated next-day air quality forecast for Patiala. The main motivation is that global atmospheric models such as the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) operate at a relatively coarse spatial resolution. This can make it difficult for the forecast to accurately represent local pollution conditions at a particular monitoring station.

I studied why local observations are important for improving the forecast. The project uses observations from the Model Town, Patiala monitoring station as the local reference. These observations can be compared with the available forecast to identify systematic differences and improve the next-day prediction.

I also understood that the objective is not to replace the CAMS forecast completely. Instead, CAMS is one of the inputs to a local correction system. Other information such as recent PM2.5 observations, meteorological conditions, and upwind fire activity can also provide useful information about local air quality.

The overall software workflow is expected to involve data ingestion, feature preparation, forecasting, evaluation, and presentation of the results through a dashboard.

This week helped me understand the practical problem we are trying to solve and the role of software engineering and data processing in building the final system.
